Before you tee off

Set your handicap and average carry for each club. These numbers drive the shot-dispersion model: tighter handicaps get a narrower spray and truer distance control, while higher handicaps get more day-to-day spread. Your "go-to miss" is a consistent one-way shot shape — like a real player's stock hook or slice — so misses always lean the same direction, just by varying amounts.
